# TETHER Design System

## Visual language

TETHER should feel like a premium relationship intelligence device: dark, dimensional, precise, and emotionally warm without becoming playful or generic.

## Core tokens

- Obsidian: `#07080B`
- Midnight: `#0D1118`
- Surface: `#141923`
- Raised surface: `#1B2230`
- Line: `#2A3342`
- Primary text: `#F3F5F8`
- Muted text: `#8D98AA`
- Neon accent: `#73F7D4`
- Violet accent: `#9B8CFF`
- Attention: `#FF6F91`

## Interaction principles

- Touch targets should be comfortably tappable.
- Motion should communicate state, hierarchy, and feedback.
- Avoid ornamental motion that competes with relationship information.
- Cards should communicate hierarchy through spacing, elevation, borders, and restrained glow rather than heavy glassmorphism.
